# PTE Academic — Templates

> Copy-paste và điền nội dung. **Nói/viết tự nhiên** — đừng học thuộc word-by-word (AI có thể penalize).

---

## Speaking

### Describe Image {#describe-image}

**Thời gian:** 40 giây · **Chuẩn bị:** 25 giây

```
This [image/graph/chart/map/table/process diagram] shows/illustrates/describes [topic].
Overall, we can see that [main trend or conclusion].
Looking at the details, [point 1]. Additionally, [point 2].
In conclusion, [short summary].
```

**Biến thể biểu đồ:**
- Line graph: *"The line graph illustrates changes in [X] over [time period]."*
- Bar chart: *"The bar chart compares [X] across [categories]."*
- Pie chart: *"The pie chart shows the proportion of [X]."*

---

### Retell Lecture {#retell-lecture}

**Thời gian:** 40 giây · **Chuẩn bị:** 10 giây

```
The lecture is about [topic].
The speaker mentions that [point 1].
He/She also talks about [point 2].
Furthermore, [point 3].
In conclusion, [summary].
```

---

### Summarize Group Discussion 🆕 {#summarize-group-discussion}

**Thời gian:** 2 phút · **Chuẩn bị:** 10 giây

```
The discussion is about [topic].
[Person 1 / The first speaker] believes/suggests that [opinion].
[Person 2 / The second speaker] argues/points out that [opinion].
[Person 3 / The third speaker] agrees/disagrees, stating that [opinion].
Overall, the group [reached consensus / had different views on] [topic].
```

---

### Respond to a Situation 🆕 {#respond-to-a-situation}

**Thời gian:** 40 giây · **Chuẩn bị:** 10 giây

**Email (formal):**
```
Hi [name], I hope you are well.
I am writing to [purpose — e.g. update you on the meeting / ask for your help].
During the meeting, we discussed [point 1] and [point 2].
Could you please [action requested] by [deadline if given]?
Thank you for your time. Best regards.
```

**Phone / face-to-face (semi-formal):**
```
Hi [name], I wanted to let you know that [main point].
We also talked about [point 2].
Would you be able to [action] before [time]?
Please let me know if you have any questions.
```

## Writing

### Summarize Written Text {#summarize-written-text}

**Thời gian:** 10 phút · **Giới hạn:** 1 câu, 5–75 từ

```
The passage discusses [topic], highlighting that [main point 1] and [main point 2],
while also noting that [main point 3].
```

**Cách nối ý (1 câu duy nhất):**
```
[Sentence from passage], and [sentence 2], which means that [sentence 3], so [sentence 4].
```

> Có thể copy câu từ đoạn gốc — không cần paraphrase toàn bộ.

---

### Write Essay {#write-essay}

**Thời gian:** 20 phút · **Giới hạn:** 200–300 từ

```
Introduction (2–3 câu):
  Paraphrase đề bài + thesis statement (quan điểm của bạn)

Body 1 (4–5 câu):
  Topic sentence → Giải thích → Ví dụ cụ thể

Body 2 (4–5 câu):
  Topic sentence → Giải thích → Ví dụ cụ thể

Conclusion (2–3 câu):
  Tóm tắt + restate opinion
```

**Template mở bài (argument essay):**
```
Nowadays, whether [topic] has sparked a heated debate.
Some people believe that [view A], while others argue that [view B].
After considering both sides, I believe that [your opinion].
```

**Template kết bài:**
```
In conclusion, I strongly believe that [restate opinion].
With proper measures, this issue can be addressed effectively.
```

---

### Summarize Spoken Text {#summarize-spoken-text}

**Thời gian:** 10 phút · **Giới hạn:** 50–70 từ

```
The speaker discusses [topic], explaining that [point 1].
Additionally, [point 2] is mentioned.
The lecture concludes by highlighting [point 3 or conclusion].
```

> Ưu tiên ghi chú thật từ bài nghe — template chỉ dùng khi thiếu nội dung.

## Không cần template (chiến lược ngắn)

| Dạng câu | Ghi chú |
|---|---|
| **Read Aloud** | Đọc tự nhiên, nhấn content words |
| **Repeat Sentence** | Nhắc lại ngay, partial credit OK |
| **Answer Short Question** | 1–3 từ, không giải thích |
| **Write from Dictation** | Gõ chính xác — check spelling, số ít/nhiều |
| **Reading / Listening MC** | Loại trừ đáp án sai; MC Multiple: không đoán |
